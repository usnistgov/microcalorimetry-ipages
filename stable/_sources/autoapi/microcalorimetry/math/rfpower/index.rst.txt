.. py:function:: openloop_thermoelectric_power(coeffs: microcalorimetry.arrays.KDCTemInd | microcalorimetry.arrays.KDCTempDep, e: xarray.DataArray, p_of_e: bool, temperature: xarray.DataArray | None = None) -> xarray.DataArray

   
   Calculate the openloop thermoelectric power from fit coefficients.

   This function can accept models that provide power as a polynomial
   function of voltage (or vice versa) or can be provided a temperature
   sensitive model of the thermoelectric if temperature readings are
   also provided.

   :param coeffs: Quadratic fit coefficients. Fit coefficients are along
                  last dimension called "deg", coordinates are integer
                  values corresponding to the polynomail power (i.e.
                  deg = 2 corresponds to c_2 for y = c_2*x**^2 ). Fit
                  coefficients can be power in terms of voltage (W/V^n) or
                  voltage in terms of power (V/W^n).

                  Alternativley, can take in temperature sensitive fit coefficients
                  and a temperature reading.
   :type coeffs: xr.DataArray
   :param thermoelectric_voltage: Array of thermoelectric voltages to evaluate power at.
   :type thermoelectric_voltage: xr.DataArray
   :param p_of_e: If true, assumes fit is power in terms
                  of the thermolectric voltage, by default True.
   :type p_of_e: bool
   :param temperature: If provided, assumed to be a temperature dependent thermoelectric model.
                       Otherwise assumed to a straight forward polynomial.
   :type temperature: xr.DataArray | None, optional

   :returns: Evaluated thermoelectric power.
   :rtype: xr.DataArray

.. py:function:: thermopile_sensitivity(e: xarray.DataArray, k: xarray.DataArray, deg: int = 2, kunc: numpy.array = None) -> xarray.DataArray

   
   Calculate the sensitivity of thermopile element using DC power.

   v, i, and e are typically estimates of the steady state values of a step
   response. Function performs a polynomial of requested degree. v, i, and e
   are assumed to be of shape (..., n) where all coordinates and dimensions are
   the same. The fitting is done across the n dimensions, and repeated across
   (...).

   :param e: DC voltage applied to a sensor.
   :type e: xr.DataArray
   :param k: (e-e_0)/p of the sensor.
   :type k: xr.DataArray
   :param constrain_zero: Constrain the offset to cross zero.
   :type constrain_zero: bool, optional
   :param p_of_e: If true, fits power in terms of thermopile voltage, if false fits
                  thermopile voltage in terms of power.
   :type p_of_e: bool, optional
   :param deg: Degree of polynomial fit. The default is 2.
   :type deg: int, optional
   :param kunc: Uncertainty on power values. The default is None. If provided,
                used as weights for the ordinary least squares fitting.
   :type kunc: np.array, optional

   :returns: **sense** -- Resulting coefficients of the fit.
   :rtype: xr.DataArray

.. py:function:: zeta_general(*, e_on: microcalorimetry.arrays.RFSweep, e_off: microcalorimetry.arrays.RFSweep, e_0: microcalorimetry.arrays.RFSweep, cal_k: microcalorimetry.arrays.KDCTemInd | microcalorimetry.arrays.KDCTempDep, p_of_e: bool, P2: microcalorimetry.arrays.RFSweep, P_dc_on_slow: microcalorimetry.arrays.RFSweep | float = 0, P_dc_off_slow: microcalorimetry.arrays.RFSweep | float = 0) -> microcalorimetry.arrays.RFSweep

   
   Calculate uncorrected effective efficiency for any sensor.

   Requires knowledge of calorimeter's sensitivity,
   This formulation is sensor agnostic.

   :param e_on: Thermopile voltage in the RF on state (of calorimeter).
   :type e_on: arrays.RFSweep
   :param e_off: Thermopile voltage in the RF off state (of calorimeter).
   :type e_off: arrays.RFSweep
   :param e_0: Time varying offset of the microcalorimeter's thermopile in the at
               the time of measurment. Typically inffered by interpolation.
   :type e_0: arrays.RFSweep
   :param cal_k: Thermopile sensitivity coefficients of the calorimeter.
                 Last dimension called 'deg' with integer coordinates
                 corresponding to the polynomial order.
   :type cal_k: arrays.RFSweep
   :param p_of_e: True means cal_k fits power in terms of thermopile
                  voltage. False means cal_k fits thermopile voltage
                  in terms of power. V/W or W/V.
   :type p_of_e: bool
   :param P2: Metered power of the sensor in the calorimeter
   :type P2: arrays.RFSweep
   :param P_dc_on_slow: DC power in the RF on state evalutated on the
                        long term at the point P2 was taken.
   :type P_dc_on_slow: arrays.RFSweep
   :param P_dc_off_slow: DC power in the RF off state evaluated on the
                         long term at the time P2 was taken (usually via
                         interpolation).
   :type P_dc_off_slow: arrays.RFSweep

   :returns: Uncorrected effective efficiency.
   :rtype: arrays.RFSweep
